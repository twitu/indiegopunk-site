Introducing Ourselves #1

12 November 2026 · Diwakar

Intro

Hello my name is Diwakar and I am the co-host for the Indie Go Punk Podcast. I thought I would sit down and write a post introducing myself.

My day job is that of a software dev, but I have been passionate about games since I was a child. My first two encounters with games were:

  • House of the Dead
  • Project IGI 2

I have dabbled in gamedev but not to any professional capacity. That being said I am very much looking forward to talking with people who have made this their career. I am hoping to get that spark of inspiration I need to make something people want to play. I hope that the people following us are also inspired to make something cool.

Some of my most favorite games are:

  • Pokemon (GBC to 3DS) - I think the original 2D pokemon has a magic which Gamefreak has not been able to capture, and there are fans eagerly awaiting something close to their dream Pokemon experience, and I hope they get it. I think it is unlikely for it to happen, but let's see how the core pokemon audience feels about Winds and Waves.

  • Harvest Moon and Stardew Valley - I love the farming sims genre. I used to play Harvest Moon on the GBA, and N64 emulators and had been craving something like it until Stardew Valley came along. I think Stardew Valley is amazing and I hope to make something with even 10% of Eric Barone's passion.

  • Rimworld and Dwarf Fortress - I have spent too much time on Rimworld, and got into Dwarf Fortress through reading boatmurdered. It is a perfect game. I love story generators like Rimworld and Dwarf Fortress, because there are limitless possibilities of interesting stuff happening in them. It also is proof that you don't need high end graphics to have deep gameplay.

  • Hotline Miami 1 and 2 - I love these games to death, I think there have been too many games trying to do what Hotline Miami did, but most of them have at best been admirable attempts, but personally nothing comes close to these two games. I love them flaws and all.

  • Dishonored 1 - I love the Immersive Sim genre because of this title. I love the stealth mechanics in this game and just the number of ways you can solve a problem. I think someone needs to take inspiration from Dishonored's movement and abilities and strip it away from the binary morality system that it is tied to. I think a skilled writer could make it work but I would love to just move around Dunwall like a shadow in the night.

  • Spelunky - This was the first roguelite I played and to this day I still feel like it is magical. I think the artstyle is timeless and the amount of gameplay you are able to extract out of such simple mechanics, enemy and trap placement is amazing. It is a masterclass in game feel and game design.

I know that the above titles are mostly older titles and 2D games, that is because I am a budget gamer and I love having the bang for my buck. That doesn't necessarily mean that I don't play new games. I played and loved 007 First Light and I currently have GTA 6 preloaded on my PS5, it is just that 2D handheld games are so much easier to play on a commute or busy day.
